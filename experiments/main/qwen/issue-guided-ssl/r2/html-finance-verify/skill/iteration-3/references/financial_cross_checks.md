# Financial Cross-Check Reference

## Income Statement Relationships

### Standard Income Statement Flow
```
Revenue
- Cost of Revenue (COGS)
= Gross Profit
- Operating Expenses:
  - Research & Development
  - Sales & Marketing
  - General & Administrative
= Total Operating Expenses
= Operating Income (Gross Profit - Total Opex)
+ Other Income, net
- Income Tax Expense
= Net Income
```

### Key Additive Checks
- Total Operating Expenses = R&D + S&M + G&A (+ any other opex items)
- Gross Profit = Revenue - COGS
- Operating Income = Gross Profit - Total Operating Expenses
- Net Income = Operating Income + Other Income - Taxes

### Key Ratio Checks (Margins)
- Gross Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue × 100
- Operating Margin = Operating Income / Revenue × 100
- Net Margin = Net Income / Revenue × 100
- R&D % of Revenue = R&D / Revenue × 100
- S&M % of Revenue = S&M / Revenue × 100
- G&A % of Revenue = G&A / Revenue × 100
- Tax Rate = Tax Expense / Pre-tax Income × 100

### Key Growth Checks (Year-over-Year)
- Revenue Growth = (Current Revenue - Prior Revenue) / Prior Revenue × 100
- Gross Profit Growth = (Current GP - Prior GP) / Prior GP × 100
- Operating Income Growth = (Current OI - Prior OI) / Prior OI × 100
- Net Income Growth = (Current NI - Prior NI) / Prior NI × 100
- EPS Growth = (Current EPS - Prior EPS) / Prior EPS × 100

## Balance Sheet Relationships

### Fundamental Equation
- Total Assets = Total Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity

### Current Assets
- Total Current Assets = Cash + Accounts Receivable + Inventory + Prepaid + Other

### Working Capital
- Working Capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities

## Per-Share Calculations

### Division Relationships
- EPS = Net Income / Diluted Shares Outstanding
- Book Value per Share = Total Equity / Shares Outstanding
- Revenue per Share = Revenue / Shares Outstanding
- Dividend per Share = Total Dividends / Shares Outstanding

### Multiplicative Relationships
- Net Income = EPS × Shares Outstanding
- Revenue = Revenue per Share × Shares Outstanding

## Unit Conversions

### Common Conversion Factors
- Thousands to Units: × 1,000
- Millions to Units: × 1,000,000
- Billions to Units: × 1,000,000,000
- Millions to Thousands: × 1,000

### Conversion Checks
- Revenue (in thousands) = Revenue (in millions) × 1,000
- Net Income (in millions) = Net Income (in billions) × 1,000

## Common Error Patterns
1. **Wrong margin**: Numerator or denominator mismatch (e.g., using wrong profit figure)
2. **Wrong growth rate**: Using wrong period values or incorrect formula
3. **Wrong total**: Sum doesn't match components
4. **Wrong per-share value**: Incorrect shares count or wrong numerator
5. **Off-by-one**: Using adjacent line item instead of correct one
6. **Rounding error**: Appears inconsistent but is actually within rounding tolerance
7. **Sign error**: Positive reported as negative or vice versa
8. **Unit mismatch**: Millions vs thousands confusion
9. **Wrong base**: Using wrong denominator for percentage calculations
