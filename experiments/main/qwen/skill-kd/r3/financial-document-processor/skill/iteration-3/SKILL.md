[RULE 002] Classify invoices by transactional structure
content: When classifying financial documents as invoices or non-invoices, treat any document that lists specific goods or services alongside a total amount described as due, payable, or owed as an invoice regardless of its document title; classify as non-invoice only documents that confirm payment was already completed, are pending approval, are internal administrative forms, or explicitly state they are not invoices.
