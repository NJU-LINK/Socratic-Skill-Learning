Rose is a classic cocktail.

The Rose has been enjoyed by cocktail enthusiasts worldwide and appears on many bar menus internationally.

It is traditionally served in a stemmed, conical glass (the martini glass).
The drink is classified as an After Dinner Cocktail.

The ingredients are: 2 cl of Kirsch, 4 cl of Dry vermouth (Vermouth), and 3 dashes of Strawberry syrup.

Regarding preparation, Stir all ingredients with ice and strain into a cocktail glass.

Temperature and dilution are important factors when preparing this drink.
