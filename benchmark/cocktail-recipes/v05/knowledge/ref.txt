Manhattan is a classic cocktail.

The Manhattan has been enjoyed by cocktail enthusiasts worldwide and appears on many bar menus internationally.

It is traditionally served in a stemmed, conical glass (the martini glass).
The drink is classified as a Before Dinner Cocktail.

The ingredients are: 5 cl of Rye whiskey (Whiskey), 2 cl of Red vermouth (Vermouth), and .

Regarding preparation, Stir in mixing glass with ice cubes. Strain into chilled cocktail glass.
A cherry is used as garnish.

The drink falls into the category of short drinks, meant to be consumed relatively quickly.
