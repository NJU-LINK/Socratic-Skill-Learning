Negroni is a classic cocktail.

It is said to have originated in Florence, Italy in 1919 when Count Camillo Negroni asked his bartender to strengthen his Americano.

It is traditionally served in a short, wide tumbler (the old-fashioned glass).
The drink is classified as a Before Dinner Cocktail.

The ingredients are: 3 cl of Gin, 3 cl of Campari, and 3 cl of Sweet red vermouth (Vermouth).

Regarding preparation, Build into old-fashioned glass filled with ice. Stir gently.
It is garnished with half an orange slice.

The quality of the spirit used significantly affects the final result.
