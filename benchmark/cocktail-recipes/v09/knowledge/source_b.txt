Bloody Mary is a classic cocktail.

The Bloody Mary has been enjoyed by cocktail enthusiasts worldwide and appears on many bar menus internationally.

It is traditionally served in a tall, narrow glass (the highball glass).
The drink is classified as a Longdrink.
