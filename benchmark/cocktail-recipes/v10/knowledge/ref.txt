Moscow Mule is a classic cocktail.

The Moscow Mule has been enjoyed by cocktail enthusiasts worldwide and appears on many bar menus internationally.

It is traditionally served in a tall, narrow glass (the highball glass).
The drink is classified as a Longdrink.

The ingredients are: 4.5 cl of Vodka, 12 cl of Ginger beer, 0.5 cl of Lime juice, and .

Regarding preparation, Combine the vodka and ginger beer. Add lime juice.
A lime slice is used as garnish.

Some bartenders prefer to use a different gin-to-vermouth ratio depending on personal taste.
