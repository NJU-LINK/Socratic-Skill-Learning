Whiskey Sour is a classic cocktail.

The Whiskey Sour has been enjoyed by cocktail enthusiasts worldwide and appears on many bar menus internationally.

It is traditionally served in a short, wide tumbler (the old-fashioned glass).
The drink is classified as a Before Dinner Cocktail.

The ingredients are: 4.5 cl of Bourbon whiskey (Whiskey), 3.0 cl of Lemon juice, and 1.5 cl of Sugar syrup (Syrup).

Regarding preparation, Dash egg white (Optional: if used shake little harder to foam up the egg white). Pour all ingredients into cocktail shaker filled with ice. Shake. Strain into cocktail glass. If served ‘On the rocks’, strain ingredients into old-fashioned glass filled with ice.
It is garnished with half an orange slice and cherry.

This cocktail is particularly popular in North America and Europe.
