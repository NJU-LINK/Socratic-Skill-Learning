Espresso Martini is a classic cocktail.

The Espresso Martini has been enjoyed by cocktail enthusiasts worldwide and appears on many bar menus internationally.

It is traditionally served in a stemmed, conical glass (the martini glass).
The drink is classified as a After Dinner Cocktail.
