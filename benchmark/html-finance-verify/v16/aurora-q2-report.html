Aurora Industrial Systems Reports Second Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

Aurora Industrial Systems reported resilient order flow across its automation, infrastructure, and lifecycle service businesses during the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Management noted that the segment revenue tables in the notes reconcile directly to the consolidated revenue captions shown below. Every monetary figure in this release is stated in billions of dollars and rounded to the nearest tenth.

Reconciliation convention: because note tables and headline tables are rounded to the same one-decimal basis from the same management ledger, differences larger than 0.1 are treated as reporting errors rather than ordinary rounding variance.

Management Commentary

Executives highlighted three operating priorities for the second half: accelerate software attach on controller upgrades, clear shipment constraints in power modules, and extend lifecycle agreements with top industrial customers. Free cash flow remained in line with plan, and the company reiterated its full-year margin outlook.

  • Automation demand benefited from brownfield digitization work in Europe.
  • Infrastructure projects improved sequentially in transmission and storage.
  • Lifecycle services renewal rates remained above internal plan assumptions.
