Alder Ridge Systems, Inc.

Quarterly earnings supplement for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

This HTML filing combines management commentary, a condensed income statement, and an appendix supporting share-count disclosures.

Management Summary

[Line 101] Management reported another quarter of stable renewal activity in infrastructure orchestration software.

[Line 102] Subscription revenue benefited from enterprise contract step-ups across logistics and industrial customer cohorts.

[Line 103] Services revenue reflected shorter deployment cycles and lower hardware pass-through activity than the prior year quarter.

[Line 104] Gross margin improved as hosted compute utilization normalized after last quarter's capacity reservation build-out.

[Line 105] Sales and marketing expense was lower as a percentage of revenue due to higher renewal mix and limited field event spend.

[Line 106] Research and development expense rose modestly as the company expanded model observability and security tooling.

[Line 107] General and administrative expense declined year over year after insurance recoveries and lower outside advisory costs.

[Line 108] Interest income exceeded interest expense because of treasury yields on cash equivalents held through the quarter.

[Line 109] The tax provision remained consistent with the domestic blended statutory rate after stock compensation deductions.

[Line 110] Reported earnings per share are calculated as net income available to common stockholders divided by the related weighted average shares outstanding.

Supplemental Commentary

[Line 148] Management believes customer retention remained above the historical model range despite elongated procurement cycles.

[Line 149] Consumption-based expansion was strongest in transportation routing workloads and weakest in public sector pilots.

[Line 150] Deferred revenue grew sequentially as annual billings timing shifted toward the end of the quarter.

[Line 151] Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments ended the period at $1.48 billion.

[Line 152] Capital expenditures remained below plan because the company deferred one data center retrofit into the second half.

[Line 153] Free cash flow conversion benefited from collections on several multi-year enterprise invoices issued in March.

[Line 154] Management reiterated that per-share calculations are based only on values shown in this filing and are not adjusted for non-GAAP exclusions.

[Line 155] No restatement was announced for prior periods, and the company identified no material weakness related to financial close controls.
